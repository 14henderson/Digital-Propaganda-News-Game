## Digital Propaganda News Game

A digital version of the Fake News Game (<a href="https://doi.org/10.1080/13669877.2018.1443491">Roozenbeek & van der Linden, 2018</a>). Designed to be played on mobile, and created as a supplementary activity for a talk at the Norwich Science Festival 2023. Enquiries most welcome at n.henderson@uea.ac.uk.
